HOW IT WORKS

🧹
Text Preprocessing
HTML unescaping, lowercasing, stopword removal, and Porter Stemming reduce noise before inference.
🔢
Tokenization
Your text is converted to integer sequences using a vocabulary fitted on 5,000 IMDB reviews.
🧠
CNN + Embeddings
100-dim embeddings feed a Conv1D with 128 filters, followed by GlobalMaxPooling and Dense layers.
📊
Sigmoid Output
The final sigmoid neuron outputs a probability in [0,1]. Above 0.5 = Positive, below = Negative.
